Your first night

What to
Expect

Rooted is a safe place to build community, have a good time, and most importantly, grow our relationships with Jesus.

Come as you are

Jesus welcomes everyone with open arms, so we do too! Whether you have been in church your whole life or have never heard about Jesus, we would love to have you.

“Then Jesus said, ‘Come to me, all of you who are weary and carry heavy burdens, and I will give you rest.’” Matthew 11:28 NLT

Open the Word

Bring your Bible if you have one and get ready to dive deep into the Word of God, asking questions to know and understand what the Scriptures mean. Do not have a Bible? That is okay. We will make sure you can follow along.

“Study this Book of Instruction continually. Meditate on it day and night so you will be sure to obey everything written in it. Only then will you prosper and succeed in all you do.” Joshua 1:8 NLT

Grow together

We will spend time together, hang out, pray together, and have honest, meaningful conversations that help us grow.

“For where two or three gather together as my followers, I am there among them.” Matthew 18:20 NLT
