Remain in
Jesus

John 15:1–17

Notes focused on verses 1–8

Staying connected to Jesus

Just as a branch needs the vine to produce fruit, we need to remain connected to Jesus. Our relationship with Him isn’t something we visit occasionally—it’s something we grow every day.

As we remain in Him, our lives begin to reflect love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control. We depend on Him to produce lasting spiritual fruit.

Letting God help us grow

In verse 2, Jesus describes pruning so that a branch can produce even more fruit. As we grow closer to Him, we learn to surrender the habits and choices that pull us away from Him.

Growth takes time. Remaining in Jesus means continuing to trust Him and allowing Him to shape our lives.

Growing familiar with His voice

Deuteronomy 31:8 reminds us of God’s faithfulness and His promise not to abandon His people.

Think about how familiar a loved one’s voice becomes when you spend time together. Spending time with God through Scripture and prayer helps us grow in our understanding of His character and guidance.

When God feels distant, keep seeking Him. That feeling alone doesn’t mean He has left you.

A relationship for every day

Going to church is valuable, but our relationship with Jesus extends beyond a weekly gathering. Like a plant that needs consistent care, our faith needs ongoing attention.

Verse 6 gives a serious warning about life apart from Jesus. Following Him has eternal significance—not just an effect on how we live today.

Make room for Him throughout your week: read His Word, pray, and put what He teaches into practice.
