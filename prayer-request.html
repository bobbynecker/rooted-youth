Privacy & Safety

Your request will be kept confidential and shared only with Rooted leadership. However, confidentiality cannot be guaranteed if we believe someone may be in immediate danger, at risk of harming themselves or another person, or if we learn of suspected abuse or neglect of a minor. In those circumstances, we may share information with a parent or guardian, emergency services, law enforcement, or other appropriate authorities to help keep people safe.
